# Player Statistics

The player module builds a structured FIFA World Cup 2026 dataset.

## Core information

- Player
- National team
- Position
- Shirt number
- Date of birth
- Year of birth
- Month of birth
- Birth quarter
- Age at tournament start
- Club
- Height
- International appearances
- International goals

## Statistical categories

- Attack
- Distribution
- Defence
- Discipline
- Goalkeeping
- Movement
- Physical

Official reference population: 1,248 players from 48 national teams.
