# SportsLabResearch FIFA World Cup 2026 Analytics

**Scientific software for acquisition, organization and analysis of FIFA World Cup 2026 data.**

SportsLabResearch-FIFA-World-Cup-2026-Analytics is an open research software project designed to retrieve, structure, document and analyze official FIFA World Cup 2026 information.

## Main capabilities

- Complete player statistics
- Complete team statistics
- Combined player and team datasets
- Official squad information
- Player date of birth
- Variable dictionary included in exported Excel files
- Complementary FIFA World Cup 2026 information
- Automated Excel outputs
- Reproducible research workflow

## Main menu

1. Download complete player statistics
2. Download complete team statistics
3. Download players + teams
4. Complementary FIFA information
5. Audit
6. Exit
